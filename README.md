### About Me

I bridge engineering physics and applied data science. Give me raw energy data — utility bills, weather feeds, battery telemetry — and I'll turn it into a pipeline, a model, or a decision.

My work sits at the intersection of three things: **physical systems** (how energy actually behaves), **numerical methods** (how to model that behavior precisely), and **code** (how to make it usable). I'm currently deepening my focus on energy engineering fundamentals while keeping data analytics as my core toolset.
